01 · Home Row Base ASDF · JKL;

Always start by finding the bumps on F and J with your index fingers without looking. After pressing any top or bottom key, return that finger immediately to its home key.

02 · Laptop Posture Wrists Neutral

Keep your fingers slightly curved and let your wrists hover lightly—don't bend your wrists sideways or press your palms hard into the front edge of the laptop.

03 · Opposite Shift Capital & Symbols

Never use the same hand to hold Shift and press a letter. Hold Right Shift with your right pinky for left-hand letters, and Left Shift for right-hand letters.

04 · Eyes on Screen Accuracy First

Resist glancing down at your laptop keys. Type slowly at a steady rhythm aiming for 98%+ accuracy—speed builds automatically once muscle memory forms.

ℹ Frequently Asked Questions & Laptop Typing Guide

How does KeyCraft calculate Words Per Minute (WPM)? ▼

KeyCraft follows the standardized international typing test formula: 1 word equals exactly 5 keystrokes (including spaces and punctuation). Net WPM is calculated as ((Correct Characters / 5) - Uncorrected Errors) / Time (min). This ensures consistent and reliable benchmarking.

Why practice touch typing specifically for laptop keyboards? ▼

Laptop keyboards use scissor-switch mechanisms with flat keycaps and shallow travel. Typists accustomed to high-profile desktop switches often bottom out keys too forcefully, causing wrist fatigue and missed keystrokes. KeyCraft trains quick, low-impact finger agility suited for modern laptops.

How do I increase my typing speed from 40 to 80+ WPM? ▼

1. Keep your eyes on the screen and never look down at the keyboard.
2. Prioritize 96%+ accuracy over raw speed; speed naturally follows clean muscle memory.
3. Use the Miss Heatmap on KeyCraft to isolate and drill your weakest keys for 5 minutes daily.

Is KeyCraft free and does it work offline? ▼

Yes, KeyCraft is 100% free with no ads, tracking cookies, or subscription walls. All session history and weak key statistics are saved directly in your browser's local storage and synced with the API.
